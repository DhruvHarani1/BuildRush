# Code of Conduct

We want BuildRush to be a place where people of every background can do good
technical work together.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, give and take
feedback graciously, keep discussion on the technical merits, and respect
others' time and privacy.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing,
sexualised language or attention, deliberate intimidation, trolling, or
sustained disruption of discussion.

**Scope:** all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and
public spaces when representing the project.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation for conduct that violates this code. Report concerns privately
through GitHub's "Report content" tools or by contacting a maintainer through
the repository's security-advisory form marked "conduct"; reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers who are party to a report recuse themselves.

This document is intentionally short and is adapted in spirit from widely used
community codes of conduct. A longer, adopted-by-reference code (for example
the Contributor Covenant) can replace it once the project has a governance
body; see GOVERNANCE.md.
